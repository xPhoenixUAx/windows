## Product model

Window Match is an **independent aggregator/referral platform**. It helps homeowners describe a window-related project and may introduce the request to independent local service providers. It is not a contractor and does not perform, price, guarantee or supervise work.

Never write:

- “our technicians”, “our installers” or “our repair team”;
- “we repair”, “we install”, “we visit your home”;
- guaranteed matching, response times, prices, savings or availability;
- fake review counts, ratings, certifications or awards.

## Technical boundary

Use only semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. PHP is allowed only for form processing. Do not use React, Vue, Angular, Svelte, Tailwind, Bootstrap, page builders or build tooling. GSAP may be loaded by CDN for restrained reveal animations. Do not use Lenis.

## Visual direction

The website is based on an architectural **window-as-interface** system:

- warm ivory and parchment surfaces;
- dark bronze/charcoal mullions and thin rules;
- aged brass hardware details;
- muted terracotta for restrained interaction accents;
- broad, readable neo-grotesk typography;
- asymmetric CSS Grid compositions resembling window panes;
- realistic residential window photography.

No blue. No numbered sections. No arrow icons. No pill buttons. No rounded SaaS cards. No gradients. No glassmorphism. Do not turn every section into a three-card grid.
