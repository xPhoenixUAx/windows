# Shared service-page system

Use `references/03-service-page-shared.png` as the desktop composition reference. All three service pages share layout, components, spacing, header, form and footer. **Only the design is shared.** Each page must have its own relevant copy, hero, supporting photos, alt text, metadata and selected service value. Never reuse a service hero across pages.

Each page contains five substantial sections.

## Shared structure

1. Service hero with page-specific headline, intro, hero photo and architectural CTA.
2. Reasons/concerns worth exploring with six detailed items and one photo.
3. Options or project types with six visual items.
4. Factors shaping the project with six informative items and provider-inspection disclaimer.
5. Preparation checklist plus full request form.

Do not put a second contact form in the footer. The footer is separate and informational.

## `window-installation-replacement.html`

SEO title: **Window Installation & Replacement | Window Match**

Meta description: **Describe a window installation or replacement project and explore introductions to independent local window providers.**

### Hero

H1: **Window Installation & Replacement**

> Planning new windows can involve more than choosing a style. Share the number of openings, current concerns, property details and preferred timing. Window Match may introduce the request to independent local providers that handle installation and replacement projects.

CTA: **Describe your installation project**

### When replacement may be worth exploring

- **Drafts and comfort differences** — Air movement or rooms that remain noticeably warmer or colder may justify a closer inspection.
- **Persistent condensation** — Moisture between insulated panes can indicate a failed glass unit rather than ordinary surface condensation.
- **Difficult operation** — Windows that stick, drop, refuse to lock or no longer remain open may need repair or replacement assessment.
- **Visible frame damage** — Rot, cracks, swelling, separation and deteriorated finishes can affect the surrounding assembly.
- **Older single-pane units** — Homeowners may explore modern glass packages for comfort, sound control or efficiency goals.
- **Renovation or new openings** — Remodels, additions and changed room layouts may require newly sized or configured windows.

### Choose the window around the home

- **Double-hung** — Two movable sashes provide familiar operation and flexible ventilation.
- **Casement** — A hinged sash opens outward and can provide a broad clear opening.
- **Sliding** — Horizontal operation works well where outward swing is impractical.
- **Picture** — Fixed glass emphasizes daylight and views without opening hardware.
- **Bay or bow** — Projecting assemblies add depth and require careful structural and flashing coordination.
- **Custom configurations** — Specialty shapes and combinations respond to a home’s architecture and opening dimensions.

### What shapes the project

- Window count
- Existing opening condition
- Frame material
- Glass and performance options
- Access and floor level
- Interior and exterior finish work

Disclaimer:

> A local provider must inspect the openings and provide a project-specific scope and quote. Window Match does not estimate or guarantee pricing.

### Prepare a useful request

Checklist: property type, approximate window count, interior/exterior photos, dimensions if known, preferred timing and main goal for replacement.

Form service must be preselected to **Window Installation & Replacement**.

## `window-repair.html`

SEO title: **Window Repair | Window Match**

Meta description: **Share a window operation, frame, sash or hardware concern and explore introductions to independent local repair providers.**

### Hero

H1: **Window Repair**

> A difficult window does not always point to the same solution. Describe how the unit operates, where damage appears and whether the concern affects one opening or several. Window Match may share the request with independent local providers experienced in relevant repairs.

CTA: **Describe the repair concern**

### When repair may be worth exploring

- **Sticking or binding sashes** — Paint buildup, alignment, swelling or worn components may affect movement.
- **A window will not stay open** — Balances, springs or other support hardware may need attention.
- **Locks no longer align** — Movement in the sash or frame can prevent normal locking.
- **Loose or damaged hardware** — Handles, cranks, hinges and rollers can wear or break.
- **Localized frame or sash damage** — Small areas of deterioration may call for an inspection before full replacement is considered.
- **Air or water near one opening** — Seals, flashing, trim or alignment may require professional evaluation.

### Common repair areas

- Sash balances and springs
- Cranks, hinges and operators
- Locks, handles and keepers
- Rollers and tracks
- Wood frame and sash areas
- Weatherstripping and perimeter seals

### What shapes the repair

- Window style and age
- Manufacturer or identifying marks, if available
- Material and finish
- Extent and location of damage
- Hardware availability
- Access and surrounding trim

Disclaimer:

> Some conditions can be repaired while others may make replacement more practical. Only an on-site provider can inspect the unit and recommend a scope.

### Prepare a useful request

Checklist: photo of the full window, close-up of the concern, description of normal/abnormal movement, number of affected windows, approximate age and previous repair attempts.

Form service must be preselected to **Window Repair**.

## `glass-seal-repair.html`

SEO title: **Glass & Seal Repair | Window Match**

Meta description: **Describe cracked glass, fogging, condensation or suspected window seal failure and explore local provider introductions.**

### Hero

H1: **Glass & Seal Repair**

> Cracks, haze and moisture can have different causes depending on where they appear. Share clear photos and describe whether the issue is on the surface, between panes or around the frame. Window Match may introduce the request to relevant independent local providers.

CTA: **Describe the glass or seal issue**

### Concerns worth documenting

- **Fogging between panes** — Haze trapped inside an insulated glass unit may indicate seal failure.
- **Visible cracks or chips** — Note the size, location and whether the damage is spreading.
- **Surface condensation** — Moisture on the room-side or exterior surface may relate to humidity and temperature conditions.
- **Water near the glazing edge** — Staining or dampness should be documented from inside and outside when safely possible.
- **Loose or deteriorated glazing material** — Putty, gaskets, stops or seals may show age or separation.
- **Cloudy or distorted appearance** — Changes in clarity may affect only the glass unit rather than the full frame.

### Glass and seal project types

- Single-pane glass replacement
- Insulated glass unit replacement
- Tempered or safety glass
- Decorative or patterned glass
- Glazing gasket and seal work
- Assessment of recurring condensation

### What shapes the project

- Pane dimensions and shape
- Single, double or triple glazing
- Safety-glass requirements
- Frame and sash condition
- Glass coatings or decorative features
- Access, removal and disposal

Disclaimer:

> Glass selection and safety requirements depend on the opening and local rules. A qualified provider must inspect, measure and quote the project.

### Prepare a useful request

Checklist: full-window photo, close-up of glass/condensation, whether moisture wipes away, approximate dimensions, room/location, number of affected units and urgency of exposed damage.

Form service must be preselected to **Glass & Seal Repair**.
