## Section 4 — Common concerns

Heading: **What homeowners usually notice first**

Body:

> Small changes in comfort, clarity or operation can help define the right type of conversation. These signs do not diagnose the cause, but they are useful details to include in a request.

Concern list:

- **Drafts** — Cold or warm air felt near a fully closed window.
- **Fogged glass** — Moisture or haze appears between panes and does not wipe away.
- **Difficult operation** — A sash sticks, will not stay open or no longer locks smoothly.
- **Frame damage** — Cracks, rot, swelling or separation are visible around the opening.
- **Water intrusion** — Stains, dampness or peeling finishes appear near the window.
- **Rising energy use** — Higher bills may be one reason to explore window performance, though many factors affect energy consumption.

CTA: **Find the closest service** linking back to the service section.

## Section 5 — Aggregator value

Anchor: `id="about"`

Heading: **Compare before you commit**

Body:

> Window Match is an independent aggregator, not a contractor. We help place the basic project context in front of local providers so homeowners can begin comparing possible approaches.

Points:

- **Project context in one place** — Keep the window type, concern, photos and timing together.
- **Local provider introductions** — Requests may be shared with independent businesses that indicate interest in relevant work.
- **Space to compare options** — Ask questions about scope, materials, warranties, scheduling and total cost before choosing.
- **No promise of availability** — Provider participation, contact and service availability vary by location and project.

Trust note:

> Always confirm a provider’s licensing, insurance, references, written scope and warranty terms before authorizing work.

## Section 6 — Preparation guide

Heading: **Projects vary. Preparation helps.**

Intro:

> You do not need every answer before starting. A few practical details can help a provider understand the likely scope and prepare better questions.

Checklist:

- Property type: single-family home, townhouse, condominium, rental or commercial space.
- Approximate number of windows involved.
- Window styles or materials, if known.
- Clear interior and exterior photos.
- Approximate width and height, if safely accessible.
- Main concern, preferred timing and any access limitations.

Callout heading: **You don’t need it all ready**

Callout text:

> Start with what you know. Additional details can be added when a provider follows up.
