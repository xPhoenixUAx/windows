Privacy at a glance

Window Match, Inc. (“Window Match,” “we,” “us” or “our”) collects only the information needed to review a window-project request, communicate about it and, with your consent, share it with potentially relevant independent service providers. We do not enable cross-site advertising trackers on this website.

Important: Window Match is an independent aggregator, not a contractor. A provider that receives your request is a separate business and may apply its own privacy policy after receiving your information.

Information we collect

The categories below describe information the website may collect when you browse or submit a request.

CategoryExamplesWhy it is collected
Contact identifiersFull name and email addressTo identify the request, communicate with you and permit a relevant provider to respond.
General locationFive-digit ZIP codeTo understand service area and identify potentially relevant local providers. We do not request precise GPS location.
Project informationRequested service, property type, approximate window count and the project description you writeTo understand likely project scope and route the request.
Consent and submission recordsConsent selection, submission time and source pageTo document the request, operate the form and help prevent abuse.
Technical and security dataIP address, browser or device details, page requests, timestamps and security events that may appear in server logsTo deliver, troubleshoot and protect the website.
Preference dataThe essential privacy choice saved in local browser storageTo remember whether the privacy notice has already been reviewed on that browser.

We do not request payment-card details, bank information, government identification numbers, account passwords or precise geolocation through the project-request form. Please do not place sensitive or unrelated personal information in the project-description field.

Sources and technical information

We collect project and contact information directly from you. Limited technical information may be generated automatically by your browser, hosting environment, security tools and email-delivery systems when pages are requested or a form is submitted.

The website uses the local-storage key window-match-cookie-preference to remember the privacy-notice choice on the current browser. The front end does not currently enable optional analytics, advertising pixels, behavioral remarketing or cross-site profiling. See the Cookie Policy for the exact storage details.

How information is used

We may use information to:

  • receive, organize and review a project request;
  • determine whether the request appears relevant to one or more independent providers;
  • send service-related communications about the submission;
  • share the request with independent providers when you have given the consent shown on the form;
  • maintain records of requests, consents and related communications;
  • detect spam, fraud, abuse, security incidents and technical failures;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and protect our rights or the rights of others; and
  • evaluate and improve the clarity, reliability and accessibility of the service using information that does not require cross-site advertising tracking.

Submitting a request does not guarantee that a provider is available, will respond, will provide an estimate or will accept a project.

When information is disclosed

Independent service providers

When you provide the required consent, we may disclose your name, email address, ZIP code and relevant project details to one or more independent local providers that may be able to respond. Those providers independently decide whether to contact you and are responsible for their own services, records and privacy practices.

Operational vendors

Hosting, security, email-delivery, storage and technical-support vendors may process information only as needed to operate and protect the service. Access should be limited to the functions those vendors perform.

Legal, safety and business events

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, investigate abuse or security incidents, protect people or property, establish or defend legal claims, or complete a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization or sale of relevant business assets subject to appropriate protections.

We do not currently use project information for cross-context behavioral advertising or disclose it to data brokers through this website. If our practices materially change, we will update the notice and provide any choice required by applicable law before applying the new practice.

Communications about a request

Window Match may email you to confirm, clarify or follow up on a submission. An independent provider that receives the request may also email you about inspection, availability, options or estimates. Provider communications come from that separate business, not from Window Match.

Submitting a project request does not by itself subscribe you to a general marketing newsletter. You may ask Window Match to stop non-essential promotional email by using the opt-out method in the message or contacting us. Service-related messages that are necessary to complete or document a request may still be sent where permitted.

Retention and security

We retain information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. Relevant factors include the status of the request, the period needed for provider follow-up, operational recordkeeping, fraud prevention, dispute handling and legal requirements. Because requests are delivered through email systems, copies may remain in protected business mailboxes and backups for their applicable retention periods.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. However, no website, email transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Providers and operational vendors maintain their own systems and may apply separate retention and security practices.

Privacy rights and choices

You may choose not to submit a request, omit information that is not required, ask that Window Match stop making future provider introductions, and reset the local privacy preference through the Cookie Policy controls.

Depending on where you live and whether the relevant law applies to Window Match, you may have rights to:

  • confirm whether personal information is processed and request access to it;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion, subject to legal and operational exceptions;
  • obtain a portable copy of certain information;
  • learn the categories of information collected, sources, purposes and recipients;
  • opt out of qualifying sale, sharing or targeted-advertising processing;
  • limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, where applicable;
  • appeal a denied privacy request; and
  • exercise privacy rights without unlawful discrimination.

How to submit a request

Email hello@example.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line and describe the right you want to exercise. We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity, locate the relevant request and prevent unauthorized disclosure. An authorized agent may submit a request where permitted, but we may require proof of authority and direct verification with the consumer.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If a request is denied, the response will explain the reason and any available appeal method where required.

Children’s privacy

Window Match is intended for adults who are authorized to evaluate property-related projects. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child submitted information, contact us so that we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, form fields, provider-sharing model, vendors or applicable requirements change. We will post the revised version and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how previously collected information is used, we will provide any additional notice or choice required by applicable law.

Corporate privacy contact

Questions, complaints and privacy requests may be sent to Window Match, Inc. at hello@example.com. Please do not send government identifiers, financial details or passwords when making a request.
